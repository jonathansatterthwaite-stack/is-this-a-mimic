Is This A Mimic(s)?

A D&D 5e inventory manager. Track gear, coins and encumbrance, pack your bags, and trade with your party over your home Wi-Fi.

Windows

One file, nothing to install. Double-click to open the app in its own window. Can host a party for everyone on your network.

Download IsThisAMimic.exe
Windows 10 or 11
Windows says it protected your PC?
  1. The app isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen warns about new downloads.
  2. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Android

Your characters on your phone. Scan the host's QR code to join a party, or host one yourself.

Download IsThisAMimic.apk
Android 7.0 or newer
How do I install an APK?
  1. Open the downloaded file on your phone.
  2. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install.

In your browser

Nothing to download. Works offline after the first visit, and your browser can install it like an app. Data stays in that browser.

Open the app
Any modern browser, PC or phone

What it does

  • Inventory & encumbranceBackpacks, pouches and chests hold things; weight, carrying capacity and fill gauges update as you pack.
  • Coins that make changeBuy and sell with automatic change, gain, spend and consolidate coins.
  • CatalogCore SRD weapons, armor, gear, tools, packs, poisons and trade goods, plus custom items from templates.
  • Combat panelTo-hit, damage, range and ammunition for everything you have equipped.
  • Party modeOne player hosts; everyone joins on the same Wi-Fi with a QR code, trades items and coins, and shops.
  • Documents & decksLetters, maps and handouts with a reader; decks of cards you can draw from.
